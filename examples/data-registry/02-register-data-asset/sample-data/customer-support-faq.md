# Customer support FAQ (synthetic example)

This document is fictional demonstration data. It contains no customer records,
account identifiers, or real support commitments. The data platform team owns
the example and reviews it when the tutorial changes.

## When will an order ship?

Orders marked in stock are usually handed to the delivery partner within two
business days. An order confirmation is not a shipping confirmation. Customers
receive a tracking link after the parcel is handed over.

## How can a customer change a delivery address?

Before dispatch, the customer can request an address change through the support
portal. Support verifies the order number and account ownership before making
the change. After dispatch, the delivery partner's change process applies.

## What is the return window?

For this example, unused goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
The support team should check the current policy for the customer's region and
product category before giving an answer. This fictional text is not a policy
for any real company.

## What should a support assistant do when information is missing?

The assistant should say that it does not know, point the customer to the
support portal, and request a human review. It must not invent a delivery date,
refund decision, or exception to the return policy.
